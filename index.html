Everything a small business needs on an invoice

Most free invoice tools want an account, put your client's data on their server and stamp a watermark on the PDF. This one is three files of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that run on your own machine.

GST without the guesswork

Set a rate per line item and pick CGST + SGST for an intra-state supply or IGST for inter-state. The invoice prints the split tax lines, the taxable value after discount and both GSTINs.

Discounts that stay correct

A percentage or a flat discount is spread across the line items before tax is charged, which is how GST wants it — no more totals that fail a CA's check.

Amount in words, automatically

Every invoice carries the total in words: One Lakh Two Thousand Eleven Rupees only for INR, or One Hundred and Two Thousand and Eleven Dollars only for USD.

Eight currencies, correct grouping

INR groups in lakh and crore, everything else in thousands and millions. Change the currency and every figure, including the words, follows.

Part payments and overdue

Record what you have already been paid and the invoice shows the balance due plus a Paid, Partially paid, Unpaid or Overdue badge, worked out from the due date.

Print-perfect A4

A dedicated A4 print stylesheet means no browser headers, no clipped columns and no page break in the middle of a line item. What you see is what the client gets.

How to make an invoice in three steps

  1. Fill in the two parties

    Your business name, address and GSTIN, then the client's. Your side is remembered in this browser, so a repeat invoice starts half finished.

  2. Add the line items

    Describe the work, then the quantity, the rate and the tax rate for each line. Add a discount and any amount already paid at the bottom of the form.

  3. Press Download PDF

    The preview on the right is the actual sheet. Choose Save as PDF in the print dialog and the invoice is ready to email.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. There is no account, no invoice limit and no watermark. The source is open under the MIT licence, so there is no paid tier hidden behind the download button.

Does my invoice data get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every calculation and the PDF generation happen inside your browser. There is no backend, no analytics and no third-party script, so client names, addresses and amounts never leave your device. Your draft is stored only in your own browser's local storage.

Can I create a GST invoice with CGST and SGST?

Yes. Choose the CGST + SGST tax mode for an intra-state supply or IGST for an inter-state supply, set a tax rate on each line item, and add GSTINs for both parties. The invoice shows the split tax lines and the taxable value after discount.

How do I download the invoice as a PDF?

Press Download PDF and pick Save as PDF as the destination in your browser's print dialog. The page is already laid out as a single A4 sheet with no browser headers or footers, so what you see is what prints.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The page loads no fonts, scripts or images from the internet, so once it is open it keeps working without a connection. You can also save the page to your device and open it later.

Can I use it for invoices outside India?

Yes. Choose one of eight currencies and set the tax mode to no tax or a single tax line. Amounts are grouped in the lakh/crore style for INR and in the international style for other currencies.
